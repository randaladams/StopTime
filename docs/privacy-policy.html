StopTime – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026
App: StopTime (com.adamselite.stoptime) by Adams Elite

This policy explains what information the StopTime game uses and why. In short: we do not ask for, collect or store any personal information on our own servers. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your game statistics (tries, perfect stops, streaks), unlocked achievements and settings (Easy/Hard mode, Light/Dark theme, sound on/off) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Reset all achievements".

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the app will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy settings" link on the Achievements screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchase (Google Play)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase has been made, so the ads can be turned off and your purchase can be restored.

Children

StopTime is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information. Any data processed by Google AdMob or Google Play is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
